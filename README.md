# Thursday 19 November 2026 — 19 Stories in One Picture

A one-page newspaper summing up Thursday 19 November 2026: three big
hero stories (the first-ever Trump–Xi–Putin summit in Shenzhen, the third
Ukrainian strike on the Crimean Bridge, and the end of the first Gaza ground
raid since the ceasefire), twelve more stories in a grid, four briefs, and a
world map with 19 numbered pins.

Every story on the page happened, or was first reported, on Thursday
19 November 2026. Disputed claims are attributed, not stated as fact, and
marked CLAIM; scheduled items are marked FORECAST. The DATE CHECK footer
lists headline items that were *not* Thursday's news (they happened on
Wednesday 18 November or earlier).

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is the entry image. `main.py` (Python 3.13 stdlib
only) copies it to `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`).
