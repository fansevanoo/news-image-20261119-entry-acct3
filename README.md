One image summing up Thursday 19 November 2026 in world news — 33 stories,
organised by region, not by rank.

## Formula: REGION-LED

Six regions, each with one lead story (full vector scene, headline, deck)
and its briefs below:

- **ASIA (11)** — lead: Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first three-way summit (Shenzhen)
- **EUROPE (7)** — lead: Ukraine strikes the Crimean Bridge a third time (Kerch)
- **MIDDLE EAST (5)** — lead: Iran hits Gulf states after US strikes on Qeshm
- **AMERICAS (6)** — lead: Trump weighs new strikes on Iran (Washington)
- **AFRICA (1)** — lead: Uganda landslide survivors seek justice at the UN (Kampala)
- **OCEANIA (3)** — lead: Cyclone Xavier hits the Cook Islands (Rarotonga)

Below: a full world map with all 33 numbered pins (red = region lead,
navy = brief), a coordinate legend for every pin, and a DATA CHECK strip
(33/33 stories verified against Reuters, AP, AFP and the Guardian).

## Files

- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 image (PNG, under 1 MiB)
- `main.py` — Python 3.13 stdlib-only; copies `entry.png` to
  `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`)
